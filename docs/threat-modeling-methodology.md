# Threat Modeling Methodology

## STRIDE

- Spoofing
- Tampering
- Repudiation
- Information Disclosure
- Denial of Service
- Elevation of Privilege

## DREAD

- Damage Potential
- Reproducibility
- Exploitability
- Affected Users
- Discoverability

ThreatLens AI will use these frameworks to structure AI-generated threat analyses and risk scoring.
